title: Sections and pages
description: Page size, margins, orientation, columns, headers and footers, and page numbers.
---

A `<Document>` holds one or more `<Section>`s. A section is a run of pages with the same page setup, headers and footers. Start a new section when any of those change.

```vue
<Document>
  <Section size="A4" :margin="{ top: '25mm', bottom: '25mm', left: '20mm', right: '20mm' }">
    <Paragraph>Portrait pages.</Paragraph>
  </Section>
  <Section size="A4" orientation="landscape">
    <Paragraph>Landscape pages.</Paragraph>
  </Section>
</Document>
```

## Size, margins and orientation

`size` is `A3`, `A4` (the default), `A5`, `Letter`, `Legal`, `Tabloid`, or `{ width, height }` with any [lengths](/reference/types#length). `orientation` is `portrait` or `landscape`.

`margin` is one length for all sides, or an object with `top`, `right`, `bottom`, `left`, plus `header`, `footer` and `gutter`. The default is one inch.

## Columns

```vue
<Section :columns="{ count: 2, space: '12mm', separator: true }">
  <Paragraph>…</Paragraph>
  <ColumnBreak />
  <Paragraph>Starts in the second column.</Paragraph>
</Section>
```

`columns` also takes a plain number. Use `<ColumnBreak />` inside a paragraph to move to the next column.

## How a section starts

`type` is `nextPage` (the default), `continuous`, `evenPage` or `oddPage`. A `continuous` section lets you switch column count in the middle of a page.

`pageNumberStart` and `pageNumberFormat` (`decimal`, `upperRoman`, `lowerRoman`, `upperLetter`, `lowerLetter`) control numbering. A front matter section in roman numerals followed by a body section that restarts at 1 is two sections.

## Headers and footers

`<Header>` and `<Footer>` are children of a `<Section>` and hold blocks. They apply to every page of that section.

```vue
<Section>
  <Header>
    <Paragraph align="right" color="#6f7988">Quarterly report</Paragraph>
  </Header>
  <Footer>
    <Paragraph align="center">Page <PageNumber /> of <PageNumber type="total" /></Paragraph>
  </Footer>
  …
</Section>
```

### First and even pages

`type` is `default`, `first` or `even`.

```vue
<Header type="first"><Paragraph heading="title">Quarterly report</Paragraph></Header>
<Header><Paragraph>Quarterly report</Paragraph></Header>
```

A `type="first"` header or footer gives that section a different first page. A `type="even"` one turns on separate odd and even pages for the whole document, and the `default` header then serves odd pages.

## Page numbers

`<PageNumber>` is an inline element that Word fills in. `type` is `current` (the default), `total` for the pages in the document, or `section-total` for the pages in the section. It takes run props, so it can be bold or grey like any other text.

The [preview](/guides/preview) fills page numbers in too.
